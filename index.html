Privacy Policy

My Finances (also referred to as MyFinances)
Last updated: 13 August 2026

Overview

My Finances is a personal finance planning app. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

Data we store

Financial information you enter (income, payments, expenses, installments, savings, salary and end-of-service details, profiles) is stored on your devices. When iCloud sync is enabled, that data is stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit so it can sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising.
  • We do not operate our own account server for your transactions.

Authentication

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or PIN is processed on your device. PIN storage uses the device Keychain where implemented.

Apple services

The app may use Apple services such as iCloud/CloudKit, notifications, calendar access (if you enable export), and Wallet-related features on supported platforms. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings.

Backups and export

You can export a JSON backup from Settings. Files you save (including to iCloud Drive) are under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
